## Course

*Modeling Dynamical Processes on Complex Networks*, taught by Alessandro Vespignani and Jessica Davis (Spring 2026). Course site: [mobs-lab.github.io/phys7335](https://mobs-lab.github.io/phys7335/).

## Sources

- A. Barrat, M. Barthélemy and A. Vespignani, *Dynamical Processes on Complex Networks*, Cambridge University Press (2008). Referred to on these pages as BBV.
- The MOBS lab lecture notes and the course slides.
- Papers from the schedule, e.g. Kermack & McKendrick (1927); Pastor-Satorras & Vespignani (2001); Diekmann, Heesterbeek & Roberts (2010) on next-generation matrices.

## Course

*Modeling Dynamical Processes on Complex Networks*, taught by Alessandro Vespignani and Jessica Davis (Spring 2026). Course site: [mobs-lab.github.io/phys7335](https://mobs-lab.github.io/phys7335/).

## Sources

- A. Barrat, M. Barthélemy and A. Vespignani, *Dynamical Processes on Complex Networks*, Cambridge University Press (2008). Referred to on these pages as BBV.
- R. Pastor-Satorras, C. Castellano, P. Van Mieghem and A. Vespignani, "Epidemic processes in complex networks", *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 87, 925 (2015). This is the main reference the pages are checked against.
- The course slides (degree-based models; rumour models) and the papers on the schedule, e.g. Pastor-Satorras & Vespignani (2001) and Diekmann, Heesterbeek & Roberts (2010).

The MOBS lecture notes linked from the course site could not be retrieved, and BBV was not available while writing. The pages make no claims about either one's specific content.

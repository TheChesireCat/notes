## Course

CS 7332 / NETS 7332 *Machine Learning with Graphs*, taught by Tina Eliassi-Rad (Fall 2025). Syllabus: [eliassi.org/fa25nets.html](https://eliassi.org/fa25nets.html).

## How this course is organised

- **Basics pages** cover the foundations the readings assume. They are modelled on Jure Leskovec's *Machine Learning with Graphs* (Stanford CS224W, 2021) and W. L. Hamilton's *Graph Representation Learning* (2020). Topics: graph-ML tasks and classical features, link analysis, shallow embeddings, label propagation, message-passing GNNs, expressive power, knowledge graphs, and scaling.
- **Reading pages** cover one syllabus topic each, with its assigned papers.

Progress is tracked in [#42](https://github.com/TheChesireCat/notes/issues/42).
